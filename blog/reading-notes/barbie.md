# Barbie

*Watched on July 31, 2023.*

*Barbie* follows Stereotypical Barbie, who lives in Barbie Land and suddenly finds that she can no longer maintain her perfect appearance. After asking Weird Barbie for help, she decides to travel to the real world to stop her owner from damaging her and to resolve the emotional troubles behind these changes. Along the way, she experiences a series of events shaped by inequality between men and women, before ultimately deciding to remain in the real world forever.

The film is interspersed with many song-and-dance sequences. At first, I was a little worried about this approach: it does not tell its entire story through music and dance, as an opera does, yet it gives these performances considerable weight. I thought it could easily end up caught between forms and weaken the viewing experience. Those worries turned out to be unnecessary. The sequences are wonderfully choreographed, and the actors' skill and expressiveness are a delight to watch.
