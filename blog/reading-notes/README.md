# Reading Notes

English translations of all six Markdown notes in
[My-Reading-Notes](https://github.com/InvalidCodes/My-Reading-Notes),
imported from commit `96b086724e5a39a446721e3d352d750af3beaeb5`.

The translations preserve the original first-person reflections, outline points,
quotations, and unfinished sections. Quoted passages are translated from the
wording in the Chinese notes; they are not transcriptions of published English
editions. Dates come from the reading/viewing dates in each note. The two entries
dated January 2021 retain month precision instead of inventing a day.
