# The Art of Communication

> Chen Jianwei · China Federation of Industry and Commerce Publishing House

## What Is Communication?

> Communication is a process of conveying feelings and receiving feedback, with the aim of reaching a shared understanding and allowing emotions to flow freely.

## Why Does It Matter?

- **Why is the art of communication important?** Only through effective communication can we fully express ourselves and bring harmony to our lives.
- **Why does communication fail?** Body language, stress and confusion, and self-protection.
