## The Psychology of Crowds

### General Characteristics

- When a psychological crowd reaches an advanced stage of organization, it exhibits a collective mental unity.
- The explanation offered is that intellectual activity contains unconscious elements that make up the inherited dispositions of a race.
- New characteristics emerge because individual responsibility disappears, feelings and actions spread contagiously through the crowd, and individuals become receptive to suggestion.

### The Feelings and Morality of Crowds

- Impulsive and changeable.
- Suggestible and credulous.
- **Exaggerated and simple emotions:** the intensity of collective feeling is especially likely to be amplified in heterogeneous crowds by the disappearance of individual responsibility.
- **Intolerance, authoritarianism, and conservatism:** instinctive tendencies.
- Morality.

### Ideas, Reasoning, and Imagination

- **Ideas:** crowds accept ideas only in simple, clear forms; once those ideas take root, they are difficult to change.
- Reasoning.
- Imagination.
