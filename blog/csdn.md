# My Previous Technical Blogs

## Sep 1, 2025

https://blog.csdn.net/weixin_45717809?spm=1000.2115.3001.5343

I have been writing technical blogs since my freshman year in 2021. Whenever I solve a problem, I write a detailed guide describing both the issue and the steps I took to resolve it.

To date, I have accumulated 102 posts, though I haven’t yet had the time to translate them into English.

In this era of AI, technical blogs might seem unnecessary, but I still believe that many of the pitfalls I’ve encountered are paths I’ve walked myself, step by step.

Therefore, I am leaving these here as a memento. Moving forward, I plan to write more structured blogs to document my learning process and creative inspirations.
